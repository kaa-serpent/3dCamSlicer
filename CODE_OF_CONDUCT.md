# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and constructive environment for everyone
who participates in RotaryCAM issues, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Share reproducible technical evidence, especially for CNC safety claims.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual-property boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing
private information without permission are not acceptable. Knowingly presenting unverified
machine behavior as safe or certified is also unacceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when conduct is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Enforcement decisions
will focus on the safety and health of the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/kaa-serpent). Do not include security
vulnerabilities in a public conduct report; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone is officially representing the
project in public.
